Something wanders the house and listens. Footsteps, doors, gunshots, your microphone. Light makes her slower and warier, but it shows you to her. The fuse box is in the cellar.

The story

Hollow Manor has been silent for forty-seven years. In 1898 Josiah Hollow locked his daughter Elspeth in the crypt and bound her spirit to a horned skull.

You are Rowan Vale, a relic extractor in debt. One night, one object. A fixer called Dacre will talk to you over the bodycam radio. He is very calm.

The rest of the Hollow family's story is told in memories, the first time you step into each room.
